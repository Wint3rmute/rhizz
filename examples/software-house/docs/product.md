# Product

The **Product department** owns the product vision, roadmap, and prioritization
at Acme Software.

## Responsibilities

- Define the product roadmap and prioritize work
- Send sprint backlogs to Engineering each sprint
- Collect customer feedback (via Sales) and release sign-off (from QA)

## Teams

- **Product managers** — technical and growth PMs
- **Designers** — UX/UI design team

## Key processes

- **Sprint planning** — sends sprint backlogs to Engineering over the `agile`
  protocol.
- **Release sign-off** — receives release approval from QA over the `release`
  protocol.
- **Customer feedback** — receives feedback from Sales over the `feedback`
  protocol.
