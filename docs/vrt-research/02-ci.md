## CI probe: 20 runners × 10 loads of the flaky story (run 39017910750)

`probe-series.mjs` records, from page-init, the ruler canvas (painted state,
size, editor scrollHeight), `data-theme`, focus and cursor visibility.

Aggregated over 200 loads (`.vrt-research/runs/ci-probe2`):

- **54/200 (27%) ended with `painted=0`** — blank ruler at capture, the flake
  state; 146/200 ended painted (pass state).
- Timeline: ruler paints at mount (+0.2–0.4 s) → **the Storybook theme flip
  (`theme null->light`, +0.33–0.55 s) clears it (`painted 1->0`) in 126/200
  loads** → 72 of those repaint ~10 ms later (`painted 0->1`), 54 never do.
- The 27% blank rate matches the suite's 25% flake rate — the same race, and
  the probe shows it without needing a failing suite run.
- The editor is NOT focused in these loads (`focus=BODY`, `cursorVis=hidden`);
  the blank canvas therefore has neither the 1 px ruler border nor the cursor
  zone the baseline contains.

## CI experiment: blur-based cursor fix (run 39017789814, commit 2e2449f)

- **Reverted** (see `git log`): a blanket `activeElement.blur()` fixed the
  cursor blink but stripped input focus rings ~60 baselines deliberately
  capture — 60 stories failed 100% deterministically (modals, status bars,
  tour pulses). This failure mode is documented here as evidence that the
  fix must be scoped to the cursor.
