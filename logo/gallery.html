0 · Molecule — exact outline, measured from the reference

src/molecule.mjs. The mark is a tree of circles joined by constant-width capsules, so its silhouette is traced exactly — node-circle arcs, capsule side lines, and a tangent fillet arc at every concave corner. One path of real A/L commands: no marching squares, no boolean ops, no filters.

The fillet system

The organic feel of the reference comes entirely from its concave corners. Two structurally different corners occur, and conflating them is what produced sharp V-spikes in the first pass:

  • A · Dot joint — the node circle pokes out past the bar, so the bar's side line runs into the circle. The fillet is tangent to a line and a circle: its centre sits at r+f from the node centre and h+f from the bar axis, which pins it exactly. Radius = fillet.
  • B · Notch — two bars converge steeply enough that their facing sides cross before reaching the circle, which is swallowed. This is every corner of the hub, since the reference hub has no dot. The fillet is the circle inscribed in the angle between two lines, clamped so its tangent points stay on the bars. Radius = jointFillet.

The previous version grew the node radius until case B degenerated into case A, then bridged the remainder with a straight line — a hard corner exactly where the reference is softest. Handling B natively is the fix; node radii are no longer inflated at all.

Note on the metric: IoU keeps rising through that sweep (93.0% → 93.5% at jointFillet=70) because it only rewards filled area. It is a good guard against drifting geometry, but it cannot judge a fillet — that call was made by eye from the sweep above.

Measurement

Geometry came from measurement, not eyeballing. src/measure-ref.mjs runs a distance transform over the reference bitmap to recover dot centres and bar width; src/fit-ref.mjs then coordinate-descends the free parameters against pixel IoU. Three findings overturned the earlier generators: there is no hub dot (four bars just converge), bars are constant width (not tapered), and bar/dot-diameter = 0.365.

Seeded variants — same structural language

A hub fan of arms, chains of which continue into a second terminal. Chained dots are placed with a clearance test, so the silhouette stays a tree (seed 4 closed a ring before that guard).
